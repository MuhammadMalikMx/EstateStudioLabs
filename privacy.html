Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Estate Studio Labs ("we", "us") builds websites and lead-generation systems for businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through estatestudiolabs.com, our forms, calls and text messages, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email, phone number and any message you send.
  • Booking details when you schedule a call through our booking tool (Calendly).
  • Project information you share while we work together.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and schedule calls.
  • To send appointment reminders and project updates.
  • To deliver and support the services you hire us for.
  • To send text messages, only if you have opted in (see below).

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you tick the SMS consent box on our form, or otherwise agree to receive texts, we may text you about your inquiry, appointment reminders and project updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

We will not share or sell your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our email, scheduling and form providers, and only as needed to provide those services. This does not include mobile numbers or SMS consent, which are never shared as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal or accounting requirements, then delete it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete your information.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in the email or by replying to us.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
